## Interpretation Notes

`quality_score` is reported as an operational workflow-generated quality proxy, not as an independent human judgment of answer quality.

The task bank was balanced by category: 10 Knowledge tasks, 10 Reasoning tasks, and 10 Coding tasks. Difficulty labels are reported as a secondary annotation layer and were not the primary balancing criterion.

The operational-efficiency index is descriptive. It normalizes observed mean quality against cost, duration, and token use, and should not be interpreted as a causal ranking.
